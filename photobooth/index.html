FAQs

Good questions,
honest answers

Everything people usually ask before booking. Can’t find yours? Send us a message and we’ll reply within a day.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and wedding season. Send a request with your date and we’ll confirm availability, usually within a day.

How does the deposit work?

A small deposit holds your date, and the balance is due before your event. We’ll share the exact details once your date is confirmed.

How can I pay?

We’ll share the available payment options when your date is confirmed, so there are no surprises later.

How much space do you need?

A corner of about 8 × 8 ft with a power outlet nearby is all we need. If your venue is tight, tell us and we’ll find a way to make it work.

How many guests fit in the booth?

Small groups fit comfortably, and larger groups are welcome to take turns. The more the merrier, honestly.

Can the prints be customized?

Yes. The Party and Deluxe packages include a layout designed around your theme, colors and names. We’ll go over it with you before the event.

What if the booth has a hiccup?

We test everything before your event and stay reachable throughout, so any issue is sorted quickly and your guests barely notice.

Can the booth be used outdoors?

Indoors is ideal. If your event is outside, let us know and we’ll check the setup, shade and power with you.

Do you travel outside your area?

Often, yes. Events far from us may have a small travel fee, which we’ll always tell you about upfront.

How do we get our photos afterwards?

Guests take their prints home on the day, and we send you an online gallery after the event to share and keep.

What if our plans change?

Life happens. Tell us as early as you can and we’ll do our best to reschedule or adjust your booking.
